Our story

Built on trust, one job at a time

Avolt Electrical was founded by master electrician Mehmet Kaan Süzen with a simple goal: to be the electrician people are genuinely glad they called. After years on the tools across residential and commercial sites, Mehmet saw how often customers were let down — late arrivals, vague pricing, messy work.

Avolt does the opposite. Clear quotes, punctual service, and clean, compliant work backed by a genuine workmanship guarantee. From a quick repair to a full fit-out, you'll deal directly with a qualified electrician who takes pride in every job.
