Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Are you licensed and insured?
Yes. Avolt Electrical is a fully licensed NSW electrical contractor (Lic. 252823C) and carries public liability insurance. Every job is completed to Australian Standards and comes with a Certificate of Compliance (CCEW) where required.
Do you charge for quotes?
No — quotes are free and with no obligation. We'll assess the job and give you a clear, fixed price before any work begins, so there are no surprises on the invoice.
Do you offer emergency call-outs?
Yes — we offer after-hours and emergency call-outs by arrangement. If you've lost power, can smell burning, or have sparking outlets, call us and we'll get to you as fast as we can.
Which areas do you service?
We service all across Sydney — North Shore, Inner West, Eastern Suburbs, Western Sydney and beyond. Not sure if you're in our area? Just get in touch and ask.
How do I pay?
We accept bank transfer and major cards. You'll receive a clear, itemised invoice on completion, with payment due as agreed in your quote.
